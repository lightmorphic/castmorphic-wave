Lightmorphic Waveframe

Turn a picture and a piece of audio into a YouTube-ready video

Preview

Drag the box to move the waveform. Drag its corners or edges to resize it. You can also focus the box and use the arrow keys (hold Shift to resize).

Add a background image and an audio file to get started

Waveform style

Your files

Background image JPG or PNG, at least 1920 × 1080

Audio file MP3, WAV, FLAC, AAC, M4A or OGG

Waveform colour

The colour is matched to your image automatically. Use the picker or type a hex code to override it.

Export

Automatic picks MP4 when your audio fits it (MP3, AAC/M4A) and MKV otherwise. YouTube accepts both, and your audio is copied into the video untouched either way.
