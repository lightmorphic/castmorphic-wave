Cookies

This website sets no cookies. Not one, of any kind, first party or third party. That is why you have never been shown a cookie banner here.

Why there is nothing to accept

Cookie banners exist because sites store things on your computer that are not needed to show you the page. This site stores nothing, so there is nothing to ask you about and nothing for you to turn off.

Nothing is kept in local storage or session storage either.

The app launcher

The grid button at the end of the menu loads a small script from apps.lightmorphic.com, our own server. It lists our other applications. It sets no cookies and stores nothing on your computer.

The application itself

Castmorphic Wave is a desktop program, not a web page, so cookies do not apply. It saves nothing about you.
